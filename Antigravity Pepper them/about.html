OUR STORY

Pepper Them was born in 2021 in the vibrant streets of Grassfield, Cameroon. What started as a small group of friends passionate about music, fashion, and self-expression quickly evolved into one of the region's most exciting independent creative collectives.

Founder DJ Pepper — a music producer and streetwear enthusiast — saw a gap: youth culture in Grassfield had rich talent but limited platforms. Pepper Them was designed to fill that gap — a place where locally-made garments carry as much weight as an independently produced track.

Today, Pepper Them is a fully integrated brand offering premium streetwear collections and a multimedia music hub, serving customers across Cameroon and beyond — shipped nationwide, with local pick-up in Grassfield.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

01

Street-First Design

Every piece is designed by people who live the culture — not boardroom-approved trends. Our garments are tested on the streets of Grassfield before a single unit is produced.

02

Music × Fashion Fusion

We are the only brand in the region that seamlessly merges independent music production with streetwear drops. Every collection is accompanied by a soundtrack.

03

Premium Quality

We use 400–450GSM heavyweight combed cotton, custom-milled for durability and comfort. No cheap blanks — every thread reflects our commitment to excellence.

04

Community-Rooted

Pepper Them reinvests in the Grassfield community. We mentor young designers, feature local artists on our platform, and host pop-up events across the region.
